# DQ-001 — data-quality experiment

**Hypothesis.** Missing, delayed and corrupted daily observations degrade the net performance and
signal fidelity of rule-based ETF strategies; timestamp-aware strict/bounded treatment policies
limit the damage relative to a naive forward-fill policy that trusts every delivered value.

## Design
* **Source**: one validated snapshot and one symbol (default SPY). Never modified; its hash is
  re-verified at the end of the run.
* **Evaluation window**: fixed; default starts 252 sessions after the first bar (warm-up) and
  ends at the last bar. Perturbations only touch sessions inside the window.
* **Scenarios** (defaults): clean; missing 1% / 5% / 10%; delayed 5% (lag 1–3 sessions);
  corrupted 2% (close × (1 ± U[5%, 25%])).
* **Seeds**: default 11, 23, 37, 41, 53 (each seed → one derived snapshot per scenario, persisted
  with `parent_id` and the full event log in its manifest).
* **Strategies**: SMA crossover (50/200) and RSI mean reversion (defaults), same costs/capital.
* **Decisions vs. fills**: strategies see *observed* data after the policy; orders fill and the
  portfolio is valued on *pristine* prices, so differences measure the cost of bad data.
* **Baseline**: ordinary clean backtest per strategy (Sharpe also with a block-bootstrap CI).

## Missing-data rules / policies
| policy | stale (delayed) bars | jump filter (>10%, confirmed by next obs within 2%) | imputation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `strict_skip` | rejected | yes | none — strategy has **no opinion**, engine holds |
| `bounded_ffill` | rejected | yes | last accepted historical value for ≤ 3 sessions |
| `naive_ffill` (**research-only counterfactual**) | trusted as fresh | no | unbounded forward fill |

No policy ever uses future data (no backward fill, no interpolation). Policies are applied in a
single forward pass; causality is tested.

## Outputs
Per run: total return, CAGR, Sharpe, max drawdown, trades, turnover, Δ vs clean baseline,
signal agreement, missed / spurious signals, refused decisions, policy diagnostics.
Aggregates: mean, std and Student-t 95% CI across seeds per (strategy, scenario, policy); paired
per-seed policy differences vs `strict_skip`; validator detection counts (injected vs detected
missing bars, extreme-return flags). Findings are generated mechanically from these numbers and
say "not distinguishable from zero" when a CI covers zero.

## Running
```
quantlab experiment run --id dq-001 --snapshot <id>            # defaults
quantlab experiment run --id dq-001 --fixture synthetic --seeds 1 2 3
quantlab experiment export --experiment <id> --format html
```
or Research Lab page, or `POST /experiments/dq-001`.

## Known simplifications
* Delays are modelled as a stale bar (values of t−k) delivered for session t with as-of t−k.
* Corporate actions always come from the pristine feed.
* Fills assume the market was tradable even when our feed was not.
* Five seeds give wide intervals; treat results as indicative for this snapshot only.
